Team

Join Us as a Student Member

Interested in international education cooperation? We're looking for University of Tokyo students who want to build something real, together with South African students.

Home / Team / Recruit

What Members Do

Members take part across our two core activities: running pilot lessons, developing teaching materials, and talking directly with teachers on the ground (see Our Activity); and building partnerships with South African universities and student organizations, including on-the-ground visits and Japan–South Africa exchange events. Members also take on roles across outreach, fundraising, curriculum design, and project management — you don't need prior experience in international cooperation or education to get involved.

Learn by Doing Hands-On Experience

Work directly with South African university students as equal partners — developing programs, visiting the field, and seeing the results of your work with children on the ground.

Travel Support Experiential Activity Program

Our on-the-ground trips are planned to run under the University of Tokyo's official "Experiential Activity Program," which subsidizes part of participants' travel and accommodation costs.

A Diverse Team Meet the Members

Join a diverse group of University of Tokyo students from different faculties and backgrounds. See who's currently active on our Member page.

How to Join

Ubunture UTokyo currently operates with 13 members as a student group from the University of Tokyo. If you're interested, reach out to us by email — we'll follow up with details on our current activities and how to get involved.
